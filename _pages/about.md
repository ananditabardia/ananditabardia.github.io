## Welcome!

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in Economics at the [Erasmus School of Economics](https://www.eur.nl/ese), Erasmus University Rotterdam.

My research interests lie at the intersection of health, behavioural, and development economics, with a focus on maternal and infant health outcomes in low- and middle-income countries. My ongoing work focuses on designing field experiments to understand and overcome barriers to preventive health behaviour — in particular, medication adherence among pregnant women in rural India.  

Alongside this, I work on three projects on health and gender in Indian households:

- **Cousin Rivalry** examines how competition between cousins in extended, patrilocal households shapes gender gaps in child health investment.
- **Sister-in-Law Rivalry** studies how competition between co-resident sisters-in-law affects fertility behaviour where son preference is prevalent.
- **Motherly Advice** asks whether sharing feedback from recent mothers with health workers improves the quality of delivery care, using the roll-out of a digital feedback platform across 135 facilities.
