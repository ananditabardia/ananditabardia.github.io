## Welcome!

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in Economics at the [Erasmus School of Economics](https://www.eur.nl/ese), Erasmus University Rotterdam.

My research interests lie at the intersection of health, behavioural, and development economics, with a focus on maternal and infant health outcomes in low- and middle-income countries. My ongoing work focuses on designing field experiments to understand and overcome barriers to preventive health behaviour — in particular, medication adherence among pregnant women in rural India.  

Apart from my ongoing work on medication adherence in pregnancy in rural India, I am also working on three other projects. Two of them look at extended households in India, specifically, one on how competition between cousins affects gender gaps in child health investment, and one on how competition between sisters-in-law affects fertility behaviour in presence of son preference. The third looks at whether giving health workers feedback from recent mothers improves the quality of delivery care, using a digital feedback platform rolled out across 135 facilities.
