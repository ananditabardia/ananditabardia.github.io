## Welcome!

I am a third-year PhD candidate in Economics at the [Erasmus School of Economics](https://www.eur.nl/ese), Erasmus University Rotterdam.

My research interests lie at the intersection of health, behavioural, and development economics, with a focus on maternal and infant health outcomes in low- and middle-income countries. My ongoing work focuses on designing field experiments to understand and overcome barriers to preventive health behaviour — in particular, medication adherence among pregnant women in rural India. I study strategies such as reminders, information provision, and the involvement of family members to understand the behavioural factors that shape whether individuals take medications consistently.

Apart from my ongoing work on medication adherence in pregnancy in rural India, I am also working on fertility behaviour among women in extended households in India and non-financial incentives to improve delivery care among health workers in India.
